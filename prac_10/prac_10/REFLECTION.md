## Estimates

Regarding the **estimates** that you did for practical tasks...

### How was your estimate accuracy usually?

My estimates were often off, especially early in the subject. I tended to underestimate how long tasks would take,
particularly when debugging was involved or when I encountered unfamiliar concepts like Kivy or inheritance.

### How did your estimate accuracy improve or change during the course of the subject?

Over time I became better at breaking tasks into smaller parts and estimating each one separately.
This made my overall estimates more realistic. I also learned to add extra time for testing and fixing bugs,
which I had originally ignored.

### What did you learn from doing these estimates?

I learned that programming tasks almost always take longer than expected, especially when dealing with new frameworks
or concepts. Estimating helped me plan my time better and made me more aware of how long different types of tasks
actually take.
