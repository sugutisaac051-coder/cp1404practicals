# CP1404 Practical Reflection

**What have you changed about your process in this subject based on your personal reflection?**

I've started planning before coding; reading the full question and sketching the structure of classes on paper before 
writing in PyCharm. This has reduced the number of mistakes I make from misunderstanding requirements.

**What do you still need to change in terms of your approach, application, time on task, attitude, etc.?**

I still tend to jump into coding before fully reading the instructions, which causes me to miss details like default 
parameter values or specific output formats. I need to slow down and read more carefully before starting.

**What are you doing well in the subject so far that you will keep doing?**

I'm keeping up with the practicals each week and testing my code regularly. Using the sample output as a guide has 
helped me produce correctly formatted output consistently.

**What do you need to stop or change in your work for this subject overall so far?**

I need to stop leaving practicals until the last day. Spreading the work across multiple shorter sessions leads to 
better understanding and less stress than rushing through everything at once.

**What is one request you have or change you suggest for this subject and/or your IT degree overall?**

It would be helpful to have more worked examples of class design decisions; particularly around when to use a class 
versus a dictionary or tuple — to better understand the reasoning, not just the mechanics.
